The Journey Behind Ramesh Kitchen Mixer

Every business has a beginning, and our journey started on 9 November 1996. Mr. Ramesh founded Ramesh Kitchen Mixer with 30 years of experience and a commitment to providing reliable kitchen mixer repairs and affordable appliance services.

Over the years, we have served many customers, building lasting relationships through practical experience, personal support, and a dedication to helping households keep their kitchen appliances working.

Today, we continue our journey by providing mixer repair services, spare parts, and selected kitchen appliances. What began in 1996 remains rooted in our purpose: helping customers with dependable service and care.
