Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: {% now "F j, Y" %}

Ramesh Kitchen Mixer accepts orders from customers in different cities, towns, and areas. Delivery availability and arrangements are confirmed for each order.

1. Delivery Coverage

We welcome orders from customers in different locations. Delivery availability depends on your PIN code, delivery location, and serviceability. Please share your delivery details so we can confirm whether delivery is available for your order. We do not guarantee delivery to every location.

2. Delivery Timelines

Delivery times vary depending on the destination, product availability, courier or transport arrangements, and order confirmation. We will inform you of the estimated delivery time after your order is confirmed.

3. Delivery Charges

If applicable, delivery charges may vary based on the destination, product size and weight, and delivery method. We will confirm any applicable charges with you before finalizing the delivery arrangements.

4. Store Pickup

If delivery is unavailable or pickup is required, you may need to collect certain products or completed repair items from our shop. We will inform you in advance if store pickup is needed.

5. Order and Repair Status

Log in to your website profile to check the status of your product orders and repair requests. This shows the status recorded on our website and does not provide real-time courier tracking.

Questions about your delivery or order?

Feel free to contact us via WhatsApp or phone for updates.
